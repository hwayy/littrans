fixtures. No paid external review was invoked. Synthetic and mocked review tests do not
establish real-model translation quality. Linux/macOS and live cross-host workflow pilots
were not run here. Historical reports remain historical, not current guarantees.

Logs, package manifests, smoke results and recovery snapshots are under ignored `tmp/`.
No push, tag or remote release was performed.
